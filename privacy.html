Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026
The short version: We collect the minimum needed to run the app — a parent's email, kid profile names and emojis, tasks, and photo proofs. We never sell your data. We never use it to train AI. Apple handles all payments — we never see credit card information. You can export or delete everything in-app, anytime.

1Who we are

Chirp is operated by Geddes Ventures LLC, a California limited liability company located at 31926 Watergate Ct, Westlake Village, CA 91361-4020. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and what controls you have.

This policy applies to the Chirp mobile app, the website at chirp-kids.com, and any related services.

2What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app for you. Specifically:

Category What's collected Why
Account Email address, password (hashed), optional display name To sign you in and contact you about your account
Kid profiles Name (or nickname), emoji avatar, screen-time balance To track tasks and rewards for each child
Tasks & completions Task descriptions you create, completion timestamps, photo proofs you upload To run the reward system you set up
Subscription status Whether you're on the free or Family plan, trial state, renewal date To unlock paid features and show your billing status
Device data iOS version, app version, push notification token (if you opt in) To deliver push notifications and debug crashes

We do not collect: precise location, contacts, browsing history outside the app, microphone, health data, advertising identifiers, or biometric data. Face ID / Touch ID happens entirely on your device — Apple never shares your biometric data with us.

Payments. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your credit card, bank account, or full billing address. RevenueCat (our subscription management provider) tells us only whether you have an active subscription, what plan, when it renews, and an anonymized transaction ID.

3How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the app — let you sign in, manage kid profiles, create tasks, approve completions, and grant rewards;
  • Process subscriptions and apply paid features;
  • Send transactional emails (sign-in links, password resets, account changes) and, if you opt in, push notifications about task approvals;
  • Diagnose crashes and improve reliability;
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, ad targeting, AI model training, or any third-party marketing.

4Children's privacy

Chirp is designed for families, but children do not create their own accounts. A parent or legal guardian creates the account and adds children as profiles inside the account. Children interact with the app only through their parent's account, under the parent's supervision.

Information about children — names (or nicknames), emoji avatars, task completions, and photo proofs — is provided by the parent. Parents have full control to view, edit, or delete this information at any time from the app's Settings, including permanently deleting all data via Settings → Account → Delete Account.

We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe we've inadvertently collected information from a child outside of a parent's account, contact hello@chirp-kids.com and we'll delete it promptly.

5How we share information

We share data only with service providers we need to run the app, and only the minimum amount they need to do their job:

Provider What they receive Purpose
Apple App Store purchase data, push notification tokens Billing & notifications
Supabase Account, kid profiles, tasks, completions, photos Database & authentication
RevenueCat Anonymized user ID, subscription status Subscription management
Resend Email address, message content Sign-in / transactional email

We don't sell personal information. We don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law (subpoena, court order) or if necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others — and only the minimum needed in such cases.

6Where data is stored

Account data and content are stored on Supabase infrastructure in the United States (us-east-1 region). If you use the app from outside the U.S., your data will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

7How long we keep your data

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account in-app, we permanently delete all associated data — kid profiles, tasks, completion history, photo proofs, and your authentication record — within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax records for completed subscription transactions, which are kept for the period required by applicable law).

8Your rights and controls

You can:

  • Export your data — Settings → Account → Export My Data. We email you a JSON file with everything we have for your account.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is permanent and removes everything from our systems.
  • Edit kid profiles, tasks, and history directly in the app.
  • Manage push notifications through iOS Settings or the in-app toggle.
  • Cancel your subscription through your Apple ID subscription settings.

If you're in California, the EU, the UK, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy rights (right of access, deletion, correction, portability, restriction), you can exercise those rights by contacting hello@chirp-kids.com. We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS), hashed passwords, row-level security on our database (no user can read another user's data), and secure cloud storage for uploaded photos. No system is perfectly secure — if we ever experience a data breach affecting your information, we'll notify you as required by law.

10International users

Chirp is currently offered to users in the United States and Canada. If you access the Service from elsewhere, you do so on your own initiative and are responsible for compliance with local laws. Data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll notify you in-app or by email at least 30 days before it takes effect. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
