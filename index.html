The flip

Stop being the bad guy.

Every parent gets tired of being the screen time police. Chirp gives that job to your kid. They do the work. They earn the time. They choose when to spend it. You just say yes.

How it works

Simple for parents.
Fun for kids.

Five minutes to set up. Works the way you'd hope on day one.

1

Parents set tasks

Chores, homework, reading, instrument practice, whatever matters in your house. Each task is worth a specific number of screen-time minutes. Set it up once, edit anytime.

2

Kids complete & submit

Kids see their tasks on their own device. They check them off and submit, with optional photo proof for things like "clean room" that need verification. They watch their balance grow.

3

Approve. Time unlocks.

You get a push notification, approve in one tap, earned minutes go into the kid's bank. When they're ready to use them, they start a timer and watch it count down.

Common questions

Answers, before you ask.

Everything parents typically want to know about Chirp.

What is Chirp?

Chirp is a family task and reward app where kids earn screen time by completing parent-assigned tasks. Parents create tasks, kids submit completed tasks with optional photo proof, parents approve in one tap, and earned minutes go into the kid's balance. When the kid is ready to use them, they start a timer that counts down their earned time.

How does Chirp work?

Parents set up tasks (chores, homework, reading, anything) with screen-time minute rewards. Kids see their tasks on their own device, complete them, and submit with an optional photo. Parents get a push notification, approve in one tap, and the earned minutes go into the child's balance. Kids start a timer to spend their earned time.

Can both parents use Chirp?

Yes. Share a 6-character family code with your co-parent. They sign up with their own email, enter the code, and instantly get full access to your kids, tasks, history, and approvals. No second subscription. Either parent can approve from either device, and everything syncs in real time.

Do I need two devices?

Not required, but recommended. Chirp is best with two devices: your phone for approvals, and a device your kids use (iPad, old iPhone, or any iOS device) for their tasks and timer. A single device works fine too, with kid mode and parent mode swappable from the home screen.

What ages is Chirp designed for?

Chirp is built for families with kids roughly ages 5 to 14. Younger kids handle simple tasks with parent help. Older kids manage their own task lists and balances. The kid-facing screens are designed to be readable and motivating for early elementary readers and up.

Is Chirp free?

Yes, free forever for up to 3 kids and unlimited tasks, including daily streaks, the family leaderboard, sibling gifting, and co-parent sharing. The Family plan ($6.99/month or $49.99/year with a 7-day free trial) adds unlimited kids and trends with full history.

What devices does Chirp support?

Chirp is on iOS for iPhone and iPad (iOS 16+). Android support is on the roadmap. The app is one single download that contains both parent mode and kid mode, swappable from inside the app.

How is my family's data protected?

Chirp does not sell or share family data with advertisers. Photo submissions are stored in row-level-security-protected storage so only your family can access them. We collect the minimum needed to make the app work. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
