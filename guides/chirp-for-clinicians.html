Chirp for clinicians and coaches

The reward chart families keep failing to maintain, rebuilt so it survives a real week. If you teach token economies, positive reinforcement, or "first/then" structure, this is that system in an app — built for the home, not the clinic.

For pediatric OTs, ADHD & parent coaches, behavior therapists, and co-parenting counselors
No clinical claims. Chirp is a consumer task-and-reward app. It is not a medical device, is not a therapy or intervention, and does not diagnose, treat, or claim to improve any condition. We make no clinical claims and ask that any recommendation describe it accurately: a reward-chart tool families can choose to use at home.

What Chirp is

A parent defines tasks, each worth a set number of screen-time minutes. The child completes a task and submits it — with an optional photo as proof. The parent approves in one tap, and the minutes land immediately in the child's balance, which they spend on a visible countdown timer. Earned minutes accumulate across days.

In other words: the token board you already recommend, with the reinforcement made immediate, the completion made verifiable, and the whole thing kept consistent across caregivers — without a laminated chart anyone has to remember to update.

How families use it

Chirp is free for one child and up to five active tasks, with no time limit — enough for a family to try the full loop. The Family plan ($6.99/month or $49.99/year, with a 7-day free trial) adds unlimited kids, unlimited tasks, co-parent sharing, leaderboards, streaks, and history. Families download it themselves from the App Store; there's nothing for you to administer.
