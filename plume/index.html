Questions

Where do the drawings live?

On your iPhone, plus a Plume album in your Photos so they're backed up with the rest of your iCloud photos. We don't have a server, so we never see your kids' art.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, add your kids' first names, and start scanning.

How does the book work?

Pick a school year and tap Make the book. You get a PDF you can print anywhere or share as a file. School years run August to July.

What does it cost?

Your first 20 drawings are free. After that, unlock unlimited scans and the yearly book inside the app.

Can I scan more than one drawing at a time?

Yes. Keep snapping and Plume saves the whole stack at once.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Plume is on iPhone for now.
