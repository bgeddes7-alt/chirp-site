Support

Stuck on something? Email hello@chirp-kids.com. A real person answers, usually within a day.

Sparrow read my rack wrong

Tap Edit rack to fix any tile. Photos work best in good light, straight on, with the whole rack in frame.

Does Sparrow come with this year's official card?

No. Sparrow comes with its own practice card for learning. If you have Sparrow Plus, snap the card you play with and the coach uses it. Your card stays in your account only.

How do I cancel Sparrow Plus?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sparrow. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

I got a new phone

Open Sparrow and tap Restore purchases on the Home screen. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Delete my data

Email us and we'll delete your anonymous account and saved card within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.
