Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Sparrow is made by Geddes Ventures LLC, a small company in California. The short version: no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, and the photos you snap are read and then thrown away.

1. Your account

Sparrow creates an anonymous account when you open it. There's no email, name or password. The account holds a random ID, how many free scans you've used, whether you have Sparrow Plus, and the card you uploaded, if you uploaded one.

2. Photos of your rack and your card

When you tap Snap my rack or upload your card, Sparrow sends that photo to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to read the tiles or the hands. Sparrow asks for your OK before the first photo is sent.

  • We don't store the photos. They're used once to read the tiles, then discarded.
  • Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train models on them.
  • For a card you upload, we save the list of hands Sparrow read from it so the coach can use it. Only your account can see it.
  • We keep a simple log of each scan (time, whether it worked, and how much AI processing it used) to keep costs in check. It has no images.

3. Lessons, streaks and practice games

Your progress, streak and practice settings are saved on your phone only.

4. Purchases

Sparrow Plus is sold through Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription, which links your purchase to your anonymous Sparrow ID.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the anonymous account and your saved card.
  • Anthropic reads rack and card photos.
  • RevenueCat and Apple handle subscriptions.

We don't sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Sparrow has no ads and no third-party analytics.

6. Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. To delete your anonymous account and saved card from our servers, email us from the Support page and include the date you last used Sparrow. We'll delete it within 30 days.

7. Children

Sparrow is made for adults and isn't directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
